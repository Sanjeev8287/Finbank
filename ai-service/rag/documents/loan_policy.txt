FINBANK LOAN POLICY

Personal Loan:
FinBank demo personal loans are available to eligible customers based on internal eligibility criteria.

Required documents:
- Valid identity proof
- Address proof
- Income proof
- Recent bank statement
- Employment or business proof where applicable

Loan repayment:
Customers must repay the loan through the scheduled EMI amount.

EMI:
The next EMI date and EMI amount are shown in the customer's loan account information.

Prepayment:
Loan prepayment may be available according to the applicable loan terms.

Important:
Loan approval is subject to eligibility checks and internal credit assessment.
The RM AI Assistant cannot approve or reject a loan.
