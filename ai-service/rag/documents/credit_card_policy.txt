FINBANK CREDIT CARD POLICY

Credit limit:
A customer's credit limit is assigned according to the applicable eligibility and risk criteria.

Credit utilization:
Credit utilization is calculated using the amount of credit currently used compared with the total assigned credit limit.

General guideline:
High credit utilization may indicate that a customer is using a significant portion of the available credit.

For this FinBank demo:
Credit utilization above 80% is treated as a high-utilization indicator for RM portfolio monitoring.

Available credit:
Available credit is the difference between the assigned credit limit and the currently used credit.

Important:
The RM AI Assistant cannot change a customer's credit limit.
