FINBANK SERVICE REQUEST POLICY

Service requests allow customers to report issues or request assistance.

Common service request categories include:
- Account related issues
- Card related issues
- Loan related queries
- Transaction related queries
- Profile related requests

Priority:
Requests may have different priorities depending on the nature of the issue.

Status:
A service request may have statuses such as:
- OPEN
- IN_PROGRESS
- PENDING
- RESOLVED
- CLOSED

RM responsibility:
Relationship Managers can review service requests belonging to their assigned customers.

Important:
The RM AI Assistant can provide information about pending service requests but cannot directly modify or close a service request.
