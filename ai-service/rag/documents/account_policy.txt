FINBANK ACCOUNT POLICY

Savings Account:
A savings account is intended for regular personal banking transactions.

Current Account:
A current account is generally intended for business and higher-frequency transactions.

Account information:
Customers and authorized Relationship Managers may view permitted account information through the FinBank application.

Balance:
Account balance represents the available recorded balance in the demo banking system.

Account security:
Customers should never share passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVVs, or other authentication credentials.

Important:
The FinBank demo system does not perform real-money transfers.
